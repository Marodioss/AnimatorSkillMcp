# Master Character Design & World Bible
**Series Title:** *The Accidental Millionaire* (Working Title: *Burn It to Earn It*)  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical Video (TikTok & Instagram Reels First)  
**Aesthetic Style:** Warm Pixar-meets-Ghibli 3D Stylized (Lush lighting, soft rounded shapes, vibrant textures, zero cold corporate tones).

---

## 1. Character Design Locks

### 🌟 Protagonist: IMAD (22)
* **Archetype:** Cheerful underdog, effortlessly sunny, deceptively clever, big heart.
* **Silhouette & Build:** Lean, athletic, medium build (178 cm / 5'10"), slightly loose posture with expressive hand gestures.
* **Facial Features:** 
  * Tousled, soft curly dark brown hair with a subtle amber highlight catching the sun.
  * Warm hazel-brown eyes, expressive thick eyebrows that convey comedic disbelief and warmth.
  * Friendly, easy-going smile with slight dimples.
* **Signature Wardrobe:**
  * **Top:** Cozy mustard-yellow oversized knit hoodie (`#E5A93B`) layered over an off-white cream t-shirt (`#FDFBF7`).
  * **Bottom:** Relaxed-fit washed indigo denim (`#3D5A80`) cuffed at the ankle.
  * **Footwear:** Clean retro cream-and-terracotta sneakers (`#E07A5F`).
* **Acting & Mannerisms:**
  * Sips warm beverages with both hands around the mug when thinking.
  * Stationary, relaxed sitting posture (no nervous foot tapping or leg cycling).
  * Big, sunny, disarming grin that confuses serious businesspeople.

### 📋 Side Character: SARAH (24) — The Skeptical Legal Advisor & Best Friend
* **Archetype:** Hyper-competent, pragmatic, coffee-dependent, fiercely protective of Imad.
* **Silhouette & Build:** Petite (162 cm / 5'4"), sharp upright posture, holds her digital tablet like a shield.
* **Facial Features:**
  * Sleek black hair pulled into a neat, bouncy mid-ponytail with soft face-framing bangs.
  * Expressive dark eyes behind stylish round tortoiseshell glasses (`#8B5A2B`).
  * Micro-expressions: Raised left eyebrow of judgment, wide-eyed comedic shock.
* **Signature Wardrobe:**
  * **Top:** Tailored warm sage-green blazer (`#52796F`) over a crisp ivory turtleneck (`#F8F9FA`).
  * **Bottom:** High-waisted beige pleated trousers (`#D4A373`).
  * **Accessories:** Minimalist gold hoop earrings and a smart stylus pen tucked behind her ear.
* **Acting & Mannerisms:**
  * Slams audit files with controlled precision.
  * Massages the bridge of her nose whenever Imad ignores financial logic.

### 🧵 Side Character: MR. HENDERSON (58) — The Stressed Factory Owner
* **Archetype:** Exhausted vintage craftsman, proud but crushed by debts, desperately needing a break.
* **Silhouette & Build:** Stocky, slightly rounded belly, hunched shoulders from carrying stress (170 cm / 5'7").
* **Facial Features:**
  * Receding silver-grey hair with bushy side tufts.
  * Tired crow's feet around kind, teary blue eyes; rosy weathered cheeks.
  * Five o'clock shadow; perpetually wiping his forehead with a plaid cotton handkerchief.
* **Signature Wardrobe:**
  * Rumpled tweed brown vest (`#6B4226`) over a rolled-up light blue chambray work shirt (`#A8DADC`).
  * Loosened burgundy knit tie (`#9B2226`).
  * Well-worn brown leather work boots.
* **Acting & Mannerisms:**
  * Hands tremble slightly when signing papers; clutch contracts to his chest like a lifesaver.
  * Transitions from terrified nervousness to overwhelming emotional gratitude.

## 2. World Aesthetic & Lighting Palette (Warm & Friendly Theme)

* **Key Philosophy:** Bright, wholesome, cozy, and vibrant. Avoid sterile corporate boardrooms or dark gritty alleys. Every frame should feel like a warm hug, even when the financial stakes are wild!
* **Primary Environment:** *"The Golden Bean Bakery & Café"*
  * Sunlit corner booth with exposed warm terracotta brick walls.
  * Natural wood oak table (`#C48B58`) with realistic varnished reflections and soft chamfered edges.
  * Lush hanging English ivy and potted monsteras adding vibrant greens.
  * Floor-to-ceiling glass windows pouring warm golden afternoon sunlight (`#FFE8D6`) with visible gentle dust motes dancing in the light.
* **System UI Design ("The Karma Fund"):**
  * Semi-transparent warm golden-amber holographic glass interface (`rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.85)`).
  * Rounded friendly bubble-style icons with smooth floating physics.
  * Soft chime audio cues (crystal bowl / warm marimba) instead of harsh industrial buzzers.

## 3. TikTok & Reels Technical Guidelines
* **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080 x 1920 px).
* **Safe Zone:** Top 15% and bottom 20% clear of critical visuals (for UI buttons, captions, and account handles).
* **Text Overlays:** Chunky rounded typography (Bebas Neue / Poppins Bold) with vibrant yellow keywords (`#FFDD00`) and subtle black pill background.
* **Sound Design:**
  * Background: Cozy, upbeat lofi beats with warm rhodes piano and gentle hip-hop drums.
  * SFX: Warm ceramic cup clinks, paper rustles, cartoon "ching!" cash register, comic gasp.
