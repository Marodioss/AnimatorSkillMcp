## 3. Engagement & Retention Strategy for TikTok & Instagram

1. **Looping Audio Transition:** The final "NOOO!" and record scratch flows smoothly right back into the opening cozy lofi beat, increasing rewatch rate (a key metric for TikTok FYP).
2. **Visual Contrast:** 
   - Warm, cozy café vibe creates high aesthetic comfort (appeals strongly to Instagram Reels).
   - Comedic facial animation (Sarah’s disbelief, Henderson’s crying gratitude, Imad’s panic at accidentally making profit) creates high meme-shareability.
3. **The Accidental Success Loop:** Viewers root for Imad to lose money, but the universe keeps rewarding his generosity by making his investments accidentally skyrocket. This fuels comments debate: *"Bro is suffering from success!"*
